# GB-202 — Reject postings dated far in the future
<!-- labels: bug, eval-set -->

**Type:** Bug · **Priority:** Medium · **Repository:** global-bank-account

## Description

Last month someone typed a value date of 2126 instead of 2026. It was accepted and sat in the ledger
until month-end. In practice, a value date more than 30 days ahead is always a mistake.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A posting with a value date more than 30 days in the future is rejected.
2. The caller gets a client error (4xx), not a server error.
3. A posting dated within 30 days is unaffected.
4. Covered by tests.
