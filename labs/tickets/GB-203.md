# GB-203 — Account statement endpoint

**Labels:** `story` `priority: medium` `eval-set`

## Description

Operations want a statement for an account: every ledger entry against it, and the total. The total
should be shown the way a person reads it, not in minor units.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `GET /api/v1/accounts/{accountId}/statement` returns the account's entries and a total.
2. The total is shown in rupees with two decimal places, plus the currency.
3. How the total is shown does not change any stored or calculated value.
4. Covered by tests.
