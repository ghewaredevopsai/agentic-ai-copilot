# GB-206 — Correct a narrative
<!-- labels: story, eval-set -->

**Type:** Story · **Priority:** Low · **Repository:** global-bank-account

## Description

Operations often find that the narrative on a posting is wrong: a typo, or the wrong reference. They
want to fix it without involving finance.

The amounts and accounts are correct in every case. Only the descriptive text is wrong.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The narrative on an existing posting can be corrected.
2. The posting's effect on the ledger is unchanged.
3. Covered by tests.
